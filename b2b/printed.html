Frequently Asked B2B Questions

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our catalog is structured for wholesale bundle packs. Contact our sales team for category-specific set requirements.

How are orders dispatched from Surat?

Orders are packed directly at our Manish Market showroom and dispatched via insured cargo logistics across India.

Can I order color sets or mixed bundles?

Yes! Each catalog item lists available color variants (saree_X.1, saree_X.2, etc.) which can be ordered as full color sets.
